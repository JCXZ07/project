You were the moonlight that lit up the ordinary summer nights. Wherever we went, your presence made even the oldest ruins feel somewhat cozy to sit on for hours while I just listen to you talk. Trails of happiness and love followed my steps whenever I was with you.

Holding your delicate hand made me realize that I want to protect you, but I failed.

As our moon hides in the coming of dawn, the sunrise symbolizes a new chapter, you and I; not forcing anything anymore as we form a truer bond not as lovers but as mere friends.
